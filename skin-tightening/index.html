What is Multi-Polar RF + PEMF skin tightening?

Two technologies are delivered together from one applicator. Multi-Polar Radio Frequency (RF) warms the tissue beneath the skin's surface: this is the thermal part of the treatment. Pulsed Electro Magnetic Fields (PEMF) are delivered from the same applicator: this is the non-thermal part, and it adds no heat.

A course of sessions is used to tighten loose skin, stimulate natural collagen for firmer, smoother-looking skin, refine skin tone and texture, contour the body, and give a temporary reduction in the appearance of cellulite.

It's about shape and skin, not the number on the scales. B Sculpt isn't a weight-loss clinic, and Warm treatments are not a substitute for a healthy lifestyle, diet or exercise.

How a Warm session works

  1. Applicators sized to the area. One console, with applicators of different sizes so each area is treated with the right one. Skin temperature is monitored throughout the session.
  2. Thermal: Multi-Polar RF. Radio frequency warms the tissue beneath the skin's surface.
  3. Non-thermal: PEMF. Pulsed electromagnetic fields are delivered from the same applicator, alongside adjustable pulsed suction.
  4. Over the following weeks. Changes build gradually over a course of sessions. Results vary between individuals.

Body and face: areas we treat

A larger applicator is used for the abdomen, thighs and back, a medium applicator for the arms and smaller areas, and a small applicator for the face and neck. Sonya recommends which areas suit you at your consultation.

Who skin tightening is for

Warm is for men and women aged 18 and over. Many clients come to us after weight loss or after having a baby, when skin firmness has changed.

Not everyone is suitable. Treatment is generally not suitable if you are pregnant or undergoing IVF, have a pacemaker, defibrillator or other implanted electronic device, have metal implants, silicone implants or permanent fillers in the treatment area, or have active cancer or a recent history of cancer in or near the area. Sonya goes through a full health screening at your consultation, and treatment only goes ahead if it's suitable for you.

What it feels like, side effects and downtime

Most people describe a deep, steady warmth, like a warm stone. You can rest, read or talk during a session.

There is usually no downtime. You may notice some mild redness and warmth, which usually settles within a few hours. Slight swelling, tenderness or temporary skin sensitivity can also occur.

Less common risks include bruising (more likely with suction settings) and temporary pigment changes; burns, blisters or scarring are rare. Sonya talks you through possible side effects before your first treatment. Read the full risks, side effects & aftercare.

When you may notice changes

Changes build gradually over a course of sessions. Sonya records measurements at each visit so you can see how you're going.

Results vary between individuals and are not guaranteed. A consultation determines whether skin tightening is suitable for you, and what a sensible course looks like.
